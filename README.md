## 📌 Featured Project

<div align="center">

### 🌍 Economic Growth Drivers in OECD Countries

</div>

> **Objective:** Analysed the role of **three macroeconomic indicators** as drivers of GDP growth across **8 OECD nations**.

| Indicator | Role | Impact |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| 📈 Gross Fixed Capital Formation | Investment Driver | ✅ Positive |
| 💵 Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) | External Capital Inflow | ✅ Positive |
| 📉 Inflation | Monetary Pressure | ❌ Negative |

**Key Finding:** Two of the three indicators demonstrated a statistically significant *positive* relationship with economic growth, while inflation had an inverse effect — reinforcing the need for stable monetary policy in growth-oriented economies.
